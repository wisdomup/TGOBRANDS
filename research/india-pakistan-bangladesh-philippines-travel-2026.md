# Travel into India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and the Philippines — market study (October 2026)

Desk research for the TGO destination guides (`/travel/india/`, `/travel/pakistan/`, `/travel/bangladesh/`,
`/travel/philippines/`), read as a senior travel-agency operator would: what visitors need to get in, what
local inbound operators sell and charge, where business happens, and what goes wrong. Searched 7 October 2026
in English, Urdu and Bangla sources. **96 organisations and sources** are listed below — inbound operators
and DMCs, associations, fair organisers, regulators and the guides travellers use. Prices are as published.

## The operator's view — what the guides are built on

1. **The visitor that matters is often Chinese.** Each guide leads with entry rules for Chinese passports,
   then for the neighbours — because in South Asia the neighbours are often blocked: India has suspended visas
   for Pakistanis (April 2025) and Pakistan for Indians; India only reopened tourist visas for Bangladeshis on
   28 June 2026. A good operator says so and offers Dubai as the meeting point.
2. **Inbound operators sell leisure; nobody packages business well.** Golden Triangle tours sell at
   $150–850 for five days, Hunza and Skardu at $850–3,600, the Sundarbans at about $1,650 for three days,
   El Nido from about $300–400 for 3–4 days. Business visitors are left to hotels and local fixers. TGO's
   packages are business-first — market weeks, fair weeks, factory tours — with leisure as the extension.
3. **Each country has a non-obvious rule that breaks trips:**
   - India: **WeChat is banned** (since 2020) on Indian networks; Chinese roaming works. Engineers need the
     **e-B-4 production-investment visa** (from 1 Jan 2026), not a tourist visa. Border states need permits.
   - Pakistan: **a formal security protocol for Chinese nationals** — police informed in advance, escorts
     between cities, armoured vehicles in high-risk districts and larger groups; approved hotels.
   - Bangladesh: **Friday–Saturday weekend**, hartals (strikes) that close roads, and a business visa on
     arrival that requires the **inviting company to notify immigration in advance**.
   - Philippines: Chinese visa-free entry works **only through Manila or Cebu**, 14 days, no extension.
4. **The trade-fair calendar is the hook** (as with the Canton Fair): IHGF Delhi and IITF (India); Textile
   Asia, Pakistan Mega Leather Show and Solar Fair Pakistan; the month-long DITF in Dhaka and the garment
   shows; Manila FAME, IFEX and WOFEX.
5. **Bangladesh is a sourcing destination first** — the garment belt (Gazipur, Savar, Narayanganj,
   Chattogram); first-time buyers do better through buying houses; factory audits sell.
6. **Heritage that resonates with Chinese visitors:** Gandhara (Taxila, Swat), Bodh Gaya, Paharpur's
   Somapura Mahavihara, Binondo's Filipino-Chinese community, Kolkata's Tangra Chinatown.
7. **Payments and phones differ everywhere:** UPI One World for visitors in India (airport kiosks in Delhi,
   Mumbai, Bengaluru); cash-led markets in Pakistan and Bangladesh with biometric SIM registration; cards and
   some Alipay / WeChat Pay in the Philippines.
8. **Honest safety copy builds trust:** Bangladesh at "exercise increased caution" (US Embassy, July 2026)
   after the February 2026 election unrest; Pakistan's restricted provinces; Cox's Bazar camps and the
   Chittagong Hill Tracts off itineraries.

## How the TGO pages use this

- Business-first packages per country (market week, fair week, factory or sourcing tour, business + leisure,
  private delegation), priced from benchmarks above and marked indicative.
- Entry rules by passport, including the blocked combinations, with Dubai as the fallback meeting point.
- Each country's trap spelled out: WeChat in India, the security protocol in Pakistan, hartals and the
  Friday weekend in Bangladesh, the two-airport rule in the Philippines.
- The fair calendar, the business geography, heritage that resonates with Chinese guests, honest safety copy,
  an included / not-included list and an FAQ.
- Prices, Bangladesh hosting (routed to Aryan) and service promises are drafts for TGO to confirm.
