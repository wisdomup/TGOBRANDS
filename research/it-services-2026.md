# IT services catalogue — how the list was built (October 2026)

The IT solutions page (`/services/it-solutions/`, content in `site/content/<lang>/services.json`,
`catalog` on the `it-solutions` entry) lists nine practices and 43 named services. This note records
where the structure came from, so it can be checked and updated.

## Sources read

- **ITDevSols — services page** (itdevsols.com/services): eight disciplines (design & creativity,
  development & engineering, cloud & infrastructure, enterprise solutions, emerging technologies, tech
  support & training, consulting & management, digital marketing & growth) plus sixteen specialist
  pages that repeat much of the same ground (website, web app, WordPress, React/Next.js, SaaS,
  e-commerce and custom software as separate entries).
- **Systems Limited** (systemsltd.com/services): AI, digital, data & analytics, cloud, security
  (consulting, SOC, data protection, cloud security, identity & access), digital infrastructure,
  emerging technologies, business process services.
- **Thoughtworks** (thoughtworks.com/what-we-do): legacy modernisation, data modernisation, enterprise
  AI, customer experience, product development, managed services, software engineering, technology
  advisory.
- **Accenture** (accenture.com/us-en/services): AI & data, cloud, cybersecurity, digital engineering,
  managed services, marketing & experience, sales & commerce, strategy, technology transformation and
  others outside IT.
- **EPAM** (public filings and listings; the services page refused automated access): engineering and
  product development, quality engineering, API & integration, modernisation, cloud (advisory, managed
  services, SRE, FinOps), data & AI, cybersecurity (strategy, defence, offensive security).
- **Gartner segment names** (as quoted in vendor recognition pages): technology consulting,
  application and infrastructure implementation, application and infrastructure managed services.

## How the nine practices were derived

The leading firms converge on the same towers: advisory, engineering, experience design, data & AI,
cloud & infrastructure, security, business applications and managed services. Those became the
practices. Duplicates in the ITDevSols list were merged (every kind of website and app is one
"Software engineering" practice); gaps the leading firms cover and ITDevSols does not name were
added (automated testing and QA, security monitoring and incident response, data-privacy compliance,
cloud cost control). Two practices are specific to TGO's clients: **Commerce & integration** (local
payments, cash on delivery, marketplace and courier connections) and **Connected products** (apps,
telemetry, firmware updates and warranty registration for hardware brands). ERP and CRM stay a
separate service and are linked from the commerce practice rather than repeated.

Left out on purpose: digital-marketing campaigns (covered by the Brand building service), data entry
and back-office outsourcing (not IT), and blockchain as a headline (it appears only where it serves
traceability).

## To confirm with the team

Every practice is phrased as a service offered, with no certification or partner-status claims. Before
adding any (for example AWS, Microsoft or ISO 27001 partner status), confirm they are held.
