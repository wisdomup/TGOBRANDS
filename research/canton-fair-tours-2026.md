# Canton Fair tours — market study (October 2026)

Desk research for the TGO China travel page (`/travel/china/`). Searched 7 October 2026 in English, Urdu,
Arabic, Persian, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Korean, Japanese and
Indonesian, plus a close read of the three reference sites (ChinaChallo, TripHappy, Visit A Fair) and
about a dozen others. **113 organisations** are listed below: 98 that sell or run trips and services
(travel agencies, banks and chambers running trade missions, China-based tour operators, sourcing agents,
local guide services) and 15 platforms and guides that shape how buyers plan.

Prices are as published, per person, twin share unless stated. Rough US-dollar equivalents use round
exchange rates and are for comparison only.

## What the market sells — the nuggets

1. **Sold by phase, not by fair.** Every operator packages one phase at a time and markets it by product:
   Phase 1 electronics, appliances, machinery, lighting, vehicles; Phase 2 home, gifts, décor, building
   materials, furniture; Phase 3 textiles, garments, shoes, bags, food, medical, office. Buyers choose by
   what they buy.
2. **The core product is 4–6 days in Guangzhou.** Land-only packages from India run ₹66,000–₹1,30,000
   (≈ $750–1,500); the Philippines sells $1,199–1,299 *with* flights and a 5-star hotel for 3 nights; Vietnam
   17.55M VND (≈ $670) for 4D/3N.
3. **Multi-city tours are the premium product.** Kenyan banks run Shanghai → Yiwu → Guangzhou trips for
   $3,980–4,340 sharing (9–12 days, flights included) — about ¥28,000–31,000, the "¥30,000, five cities,
   visa and living covered" price point. Shorter variants add Foshan, Dongguan and Shenzhen as day trips.
4. **Two-phase combos fill the changeover.** There are three days between phases; Cosima (India) sells an
   8N/9D two-phase tour (₹1,15,000) and fills the gap with sightseeing. Factories are a better use of it.
5. **What wins the sale** (repeated across markets): visa handled end to end (invitation letter, group visa,
   "no appearance"); a hotel near Pazhou or a shuttle; daily transfers; badge pre-registration; food from
   home (Indian dinners, halal); an interpreter in the buyer's language (Urdu, Hindi, Arabic); a tour manager
   from the buyer's own country; SIM and payment apps set up; insurance.
6. **What separates the best:** a pre-trip strategy call, a pre-screened supplier shortlist, factory meetings
   booked before arrival, and follow-up after the trip — samples, factory checks, QC, shipping (ChinaChallo,
   Leeline, Asian Sourcing Group, HSY).
7. **Hidden costs buyers resent:** flights quoted separately; visa sticker fees; GST/TCS in India (5% + 5%);
   Philippine travel tax; lunches on fair days; tips; single supplements; a separate badge for a local
   interpreter. Cosima's "landed cost" table — package + tax + airfare = all-in — is the trust move.
8. **Trust signals:** years in business, Google rating, ISO, video testimonials from named cities, past
   delegation photos, a buyer-protection or escrow-style payment plan (25% deposit, balance 30 days before),
   clear cancellation tiers (ChinaChallo: 60+ days full refund, 30–60 days 50%, under 30 days none), and
   institutions — banks (KCB, NCBA, Co-op, OmniBSIC) and chambers (Lima, Oman, ACCCIM, LCCI) lend
   credibility and bring the groups.
9. **Urgency is engineered** with deposit deadlines (KCB: 15 August for an October trip; OmniBSIC:
   10 September) and "limited seats".
10. **The gap TGO fills:** travel agents handle logistics only; sourcing agents work only on the China side.
    Almost nobody also checks that what you buy can be **certified and cleared at home** (BIS, PSQCA, BPS,
    ECAS) or helps you **sell it** after the container lands. TGO has teams in the buyer's market and in China.

## Facts used on the page

- **140th Canton Fair (autumn 2026):** Phase 1 15–19 Oct, Phase 2 23–27 Oct, Phase 3 31 Oct–4 Nov,
  China Import and Export Fair Complex, Pazhou, Guangzhou.
- **141st (spring 2027):** 15 Apr–5 May 2027, phases 15–19 Apr, 23–27 Apr, 1–5 May — expected, to confirm.
- **Registration:** pre-register online (free); it issues the invitation letter used for the business visa;
  bring the passport you registered with and business cards. Badging on site costs more and queues are long.
- **Visas:** Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, the Philippines, Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana need a visa —
  usually the M (business) visa with the fair's invitation letter. For the 140th fair the Chinese Embassy in
  Dhaka made the invitation letter the only mandatory document for Bangladeshi applicants. Philippine groups
  often use a group visa through an accredited agency. UAE and Qatar passports enter visa-free for 30 days;
  in 2026 about 50 countries (much of Europe, the UK and Canada from February 2026, and others) have 30-day
  visa-free entry. Rules change — check per passport.
- **Yiwu:** International Trade City, 75,000+ booths. **Foshan:** 45–60 minutes from Guangzhou.
